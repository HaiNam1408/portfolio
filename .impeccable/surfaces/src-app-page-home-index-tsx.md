# Surface: Home (single-page portfolio)

Mode: Experience. The work (Nam's record and projects) leads from the first viewport.

Audience: recruiters skimming, tech leads verifying, freelance clients. Actions: download CV, open a project, send a message. Constraints: keep AI chatbot, EmailJS form, EN/VI, light/dark, all current copy and projects.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is Nam's passport: the data page is his identity, visas are projects, entry stamps are employers and honors. Refuses the dark dev template (gradient name, tilted avatar, logo marquee, equal card grid).

OWN-WORLD: Light: security-paper ivory ground with rose/green guilloché hairlines, burgundy cover leather rail with pressed-gold crest, ink navy text, stamp inks (red, navy, green, plum) only on stamps. Dark: the closed cover, burgundy-black leather, gold-blocked type, pages dimmed. Engraved high-contrast serif display, OCR-style mono for data fields and MRZ, ruled field rows.

STORY: Visitor meets a passport data page, reads who Nam is and where he has worked from real stamps, flips through visa plates for each project, then fills a document-style application to contact him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left leather rail (nav, language, theme). Center-left: name set huge in engraved serif, role line, intro, primary burgundy "Download resume" plus secondary "Contact". Right: the data page card (photo, ruled fields, MRZ, hologram seal) with Kozocom and KDHM stamps landing on it.

FORM: Passport (vernacular-ephemera-well-traveled-passport), first-roll challenger adopted by user pin; seed:38de2d7144e05a39. Signature interaction: stamps land with one press, then stay still. Motion grammar: press-in only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
